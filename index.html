Featured Programs

Four doors in, one circle around every child.

Each program stands on its own and folds into the others — literacy strengthens wellness, wellness opens creativity, creativity brings families back together.

Aa

Reading Circles

Small-group literacy mentorship where children choose the books, set the pace, and read out loud without fear of getting it wrong.

◒

Wellness & Movement

Breathwork, yoga, and free movement sessions designed to give children language for their bodies and their feelings.

✎

Creative Studio

Open studio time in art, music, and writing — no grading, no critique, just the practice of making something true.

⌂

Family Table

Monthly gatherings that bring parents, grandparents, and guardians into the same room the children already call home.

✳

Mentor Match

One-on-one pairings with trained community mentors who show up, week after week, for as long as it takes.

☀

Summer Bloom

A six-week intensive that blends every program into one season, so no child loses ground when school lets out.
